Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PSCI Pro handles information. Our primary commitment is to ensure that no personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted from your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect, process, or store any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (Name, Email, Phone Number)
  • Location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, or Cell tower)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC Address, Android ID)
  • Contact list or calendar data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

Permissions

PSCI Pro operates entirely locally on your device. It does not require any sensitive permissions such as storage, internet, or camera access unless explicitly mandated for a specific feature (which is not the case in this version).

Third-Party Services

We do not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social media SDKs. Consequently, no data is shared with any external entities.

Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected, we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. If we do, we will post the new policy on this page. Since we do not collect personal data, changes to this policy are strictly informational.
